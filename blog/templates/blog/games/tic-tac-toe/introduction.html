Welcome to Infinite Tic-Tac-Toe

Ready for an expanded challenge? Infinite Tic-Tac-Toe takes the classic game to a boundless board. Can you outsmart your opponent on an endless field?

How It Works

Two players take turns marking spaces in an infinite grid with X and O.

The player who succeeds in placing five of their marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal row is the winner.
